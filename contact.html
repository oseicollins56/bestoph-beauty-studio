Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the things our clients ask most often.

Do I need to book in advance?
Yes, we recommend booking at least 2–3 days in advance to secure your preferred date and time, especially on weekends. Walk-ins are welcome based on availability.
Do you offer home service visits?
Yes! We offer home visits for bridal and event makeup. Please contact us directly to discuss availability and any additional travel fees.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept cash, Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash, AirtelTigo), and bank transfers. Card payments may be available — please enquire when booking.
How long do services take?
It depends on the service. Simple styles like cornrows may take 1–2 hours, while knotless braids or full bridal packages can take 4–6 hours. We'll give you an estimate at booking.
Can I bring my own hair extensions or products?
Absolutely! You're welcome to bring your own extensions, weave, or products. We can also supply them for an additional fee if needed.
