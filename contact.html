Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to book in advance?+
Yes, we recommend booking 2–3 days in advance to secure your preferred date and time. Walk-ins are welcome subject to availability.
Do you offer home or on-location service?+
Yes! We offer home visits for bridal makeup and certain services. Please contact us directly to discuss availability and travel fees.
What payment methods do you accept?+
We accept cash and Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash, AirtelTigo). Please enquire about card payments when booking.
How long do lash extensions last?+
Lash extensions typically last 3–4 weeks with proper care. We recommend infills every 2–3 weeks to keep them looking full and fresh.
What is microblading and how long does it last?+
Microblading is a semi-permanent eyebrow technique that creates natural-looking hair strokes. Results last 12–18 months depending on skin type and aftercare. A touch-up at 4–6 weeks is recommended.
Can I supply my own hair for wig making?+
Absolutely! You're welcome to supply your own bundles and closure/frontal. We can also source the hair for you at an additional cost.
